Business Performance Analytics

About This Repository

This repository showcases my work in data analysis, business intelligence, and financial performance reporting using Microsoft Excel and Power BI.

My professional experience includes analysing financial and operational performance across five business subsidiaries operating in lending, digital savings, renewable energy, and asset management.

Areas of Focus

* Financial Analytics: Loan portfolio performance, disbursements, and portfolio at risk (PAR).
* Customer Analytics: Onboarding, customer drop-offs, deposits, and transaction activity.
* Sales & Profitability: Revenue, costs, profit margins, and sales performance.
* Business Intelligence: KPI monitoring, dashboards, and performance reporting.
* Data Preparation: Data cleaning, validation, transformation, and reporting.
